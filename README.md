# Trading Journal System — free beta

A spreadsheet trading journal for futures, prop-firm evals and crypto. It logs your trades, then builds the dashboard, account tracking and stats for you.

**This is a one-week beta.** It's free. In exchange, tell me what's confusing, what breaks and what you'd want added.

## What's inside

- **Trade Log**: one row per trade. P&L, R-multiple, fees and running balance are calculated for you.
- **Dashboard**: net P&L, win rate, profit factor, equity curve, and P&L by setup, weekday and emotion.
- **Accounts**: tracks several accounts at once (prop evals, funded, personal, crypto), including drawdown floor and room left for trailing or static drawdown rules.
- **Setup Playbook, Daily Journal, Weekly Review, Psychology**: the parts that actually make you better.
- **Trader modes** in Settings: switch between futures, prop-firm, crypto and other styles.

## Getting started

1. Open the file in **Excel 2019 or newer / Microsoft 365**.
   - **Google Sheets:** upload it to Google Drive, then right-click it → *Open with* → *Google Sheets*.
2. If Excel shows a yellow *Protected View* bar, click **Enable Editing**.
3. Go through the **Start Here** tab. It walks you through setup and clearing the sample trades.

The file opens with sample trades so you can see how everything works before you add your own.
